Privacy

The app phones no one home.

What the app never does

  • No analytics — no usage telemetry, no event tracking, no A/B frameworks.
  • No crash reporting — no Sentry, no Crashlytics, nothing.
  • No accounts — no email, no sign-in, no user id uploaded anywhere.
  • No advertising — no ad SDKs, no attribution, no trackers.

All of your study data — review history, FSRS memory states, practice accuracy, known words, settings — lives in two SQLite databases on your device: the app's store (your progress) and the bundled morphology database (read-only reference data, shipped with the app).

Network access

The app makes no network requests during normal use. Textbook cards and the 113 MB morphology database are bundled with the APK and consulted locally. The only time the app touches the network is if you go to Settings and configure device sync — and even then it talks only to the destination you configured: your chosen nostr relay, or a device you paired by QR code (off-LAN, that peer-to-peer connection is bootstrapped through iroh's default DNS/n0 discovery relays, which are transport pipes, not storage).

If you enable sync: what a relay can and cannot see

Sync payloads on the nostr transport are NIP-44 end-to-end encrypted to yourself, and direct sync over iroh is QUIC/TLS between your own devices. Assume a hostile relay or a network observer anyway — here's the honest accounting:

Visible to a relay / observerNever visible
Encrypted blobs (ciphertext) of sync batchesWhat you studied — card ids, Greek text of study items, ratings, schedule — all inside the ciphertext
Your sync public key (written under it)Your settings values (also encrypted)
Timing: when your devices publish/fetchThe checkpoint label — d-tags are HMAC-blinded, so even the existence and cadence of sync metadata is hidden
Count and size of events (chunking makes an educated guess of total study volume possible)
The greek-sync topic tag and a device id on chunk events

Timing-plus-size metadata is the accepted residual leak in v1 (documented in SYNC-WIRE.md §2.3). If that's too much, your options, strongest first: don't enable sync at all, use only the local JSON export for backup, use QR-paired direct sync between your own devices on your own network, or run your own relay (the runbook is 10 lines of strfry + nginx, write-whitelisted to your key).

Data sources and credit

Morphological data is derived from Morpheus (the Perseus morphology/lemmatizer XML output — part of the Perseus Digital Library's openly licensed data; Perseus texts and morphology are CC BY-SA). Dictionary glosses in the app are Morpheus short glosses with a version-controlled set of human corrections (data/overrides/lemma_glosses.tsv) applied on top; full provenance, warts included, is in the repository at data/README.md. The LSJ Greek–English lexicon (1940) is public domain but is not yet ingested — the app's dictionary is labelled "Morpheus glosses (LSJ pending)" and will say so until real LSJ entries ship.

The app's own source repository does not currently carry a LICENSE file; project notes express intent to license the code permissively (MIT/Apache-2.0). Treat all app code as all-rights-reserved until the explicit license lands.
